## Confirmed against current official checklist
All 18 entries marked eligible in scripts/circulation-master.json are present in Browse. No proven missing release found among this selected verified set. This is not an exhaustive 2016–2026 reconciliation.

## Eligibility resolutions
- 2023 Aussie Big Things: Australia Post explicitly offered the coins as cash change. The ten Browse records are appropriate.
- 2025 Aussie Big Things 2: ten individually sold designs should remain outside the change-only official catalogue; both additional exclusive till-change designs appear in Browse. Existing placeholders 'Till Design 1/2' require authentic design titles and images.
- 2024 Bluey and Bingo $1: Royal Australian Mint describes them under uncirculated partnerships, not its circulating denomination register; retained pending a deliberate policy ruling rather than silently deleting collection entries.
- Fremantle Dockers collector coin: no validated circulating-change release; continue to exclude.
